DownhillSpotter Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

DownhillSpotter ("the app") helps downhill skateboarders spot road traffic. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

What the app uses

  • Camera: video frames are analysed on your device to detect road users. Video is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: used to hear voice commands (such as "clear") from your walkie-talkie. Speech recognition is performed by Apple's speech service on your device or via Apple, under Apple's privacy policy. The app does not store audio or transcripts.
  • Settings: your timing preferences and chosen road area are stored only on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising identifier and similar data to show ads, if you allow tracking when asked. You can decline the tracking request or change it at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

What we do not do

We do not collect your name, location or contact details, create accounts, or sell your data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change.
